Nail Sizing

How to Take Sizing Photos

Follow these steps to measure your nails perfectly:

  1. Place your hand on a flat surface and relax your fingers.
  2. Have a US quarter (or similar coin) next to your 4 fingers for scale.
  3. Take a photo from directly above so the camera is not angled.
  4. Repeat for your thumb.
  5. DM the photos to Instagram with: full name, preferred nail length, and order reference (if you have one).

Once I have your sizes, they’ll be saved under your name so repeat orders are quick and easy.
